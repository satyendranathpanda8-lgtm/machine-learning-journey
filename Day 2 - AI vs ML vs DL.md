# Day 2: AI vs ML vs DL

## 1. Artificial Intelligence (AI)

- AI is about making computers or machines smart enough to do things that usually need human thinking.
- **Examples:** Siri, Alexa, Netflix recommendations
- **Big picture:** AI is the main field, and ML and DL are parts of it.

## 2. Machine Learning (ML)

- ML is a part of AI that helps computers learn from data and improve on their own, without being told exactly what to do.

### Types of ML

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Supervised** | Learn from examples with answers |
| **Unsupervised** | Find patterns in data without answers |
| **Reinforcement** | Learn by trying things and seeing what works best |

- **Examples:** Email spam filters, fraud detection, predicting trends

---

## 3. Deep Learning (DL)

- DL is a special type of ML that uses **neural networks** (like a computer brain) to solve very complex problems.

### Key Points

- Needs a lot of data
- Needs powerful computers
- Very good at things like recognizing images, voices, or understanding a language

- **Examples:** Face recognition, voice assistants, self-driving cars

---
